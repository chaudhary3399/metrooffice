Privacy

Privacy policy

MetroOffice Shuttle uses the information you provide to process shuttle bookings and support your commute.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, WhatsApp phone number, selected route, departure time, seat details, and payment reference when you make a booking.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your booking.
  • To send booking, payment, and service updates.
  • To provide customer support and resolve disputes.
  • To meet legal, accounting, and payment-provider requirements.

Sharing

We share only the information needed with service operators and payment providers to deliver the booking. We do not sell customer information.

Retention and contact

We retain booking records for operational, accounting, and legal purposes. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the support address shown on our Contact page.
